# Reading status panel

**Working-tree note, 2026-09-05:** The owner rejected the first hand-drawn motion pass ("Reading Lens") on sight and asked for a complete redesign that is super aesthetic, satisfying and high quality, with far more satisfying sounds. The design below is that second pass. The verification record at the end lists what was actually run against it; the older record applies to the earlier panel and is kept as history.

Implemented on 2026-09-05 following the owner's approval of a compact, branded reading control.

## Behavior and design

- `AppModel` supplies status and an ephemeral scalar playback level only, through `ReadingStatusPresenting`. No screenshot, selected text, audio, or arbitrary diagnostic payload enters the panel.
- The panel stays hidden during selection and capture. Recognition, preparation and playback appear in a 376 × 100 pt nonactivating AppKit panel on the selected display; the visible 312 × 60 pt body sits 28 pt above the bottom of the visible frame and the remaining surface is transparent room for the drawn shadow. Missing displays hide the panel instead of moving it to an unrelated screen.
- A round 44 × 44 pt button cancels the same full pipeline as Escape and the repeated hotkey. Its callback is scoped to its operation; stale buttons cannot cancel a newer selection. Presentation generations also invalidate old dismissal timers. Pressing it compresses only its visible face, fires a haptic and a short click, and the hit area never moves.
- Completed and stopped states remain for 1.1 seconds, then the body closes into a dot and fades within 240 ms. Empty-text and error notices stay until explicitly dismissed or a new selection starts. Errors direct users to the existing menu details; no extra confirmation is introduced before speech. Selection/capture always remove the panel immediately, never after an exit animation.
- Material: a translucent Local Ink tint over a `hudWindow` system blur, a depth gradient with a fine Warm Paper top highlight, a hairline outline and two drawn shadows. The approved owl artwork and menu-bar glyph are unchanged. A gradient Voice Mint ribbon with halo and radial glow follows the rendered speech level (fast attack, slow release); recognition shows a travelling light on a flat line, preparation a calm swell. Terminal states draw a mint check, a paper stop square or a warm amber attention ring while the text slides left; completion adds a brief body pulse. The body opens from a 60 pt dot with one spring (about 400 ms, slight overshoot) and closes critically damped. Reduce Motion removes spring, travelling light, self-drawing, pulse and press scaling; Reduce Transparency makes the body opaque; Increase Contrast strengthens the outline.
- Four original deterministic mono cues are synthesized in memory at launch from one family (filtered-noise strike, additive harmonic body with per-partial decay, raised-cosine tail): opening after capture (420 ms), completion (760 ms), attention (460 ms) and the stop-button tap (60 ms). Peaks are normalised to 0.5 (tap 0.42) before the 60 % default effect volume. Enabled and volume persist as one Boolean and one number. Playback start and cancellation stop any cue; the tap is never registered as an interruptible cue, so cancellation cannot clip it. Sounds never block the pipeline and do not use the speech model, files or network.
- The panel is neither key nor main, does not activate the app, and accepts the first stop click while another app is foreground. It joins Spaces and can accompany full-screen apps. ScreenCaptureKit already excludes all windows belonging to OpenScreenReader; hiding before capture is the primary presentation rule, not a reliance on window-sharing flags.

### Design review export

`TEST_RUNNER_OSR_PANEL_PREVIEW_DIR=/some/dir` with `-only-testing:OpenScreenReaderAppTests/ReadingPanelTests` writes the four cue WAVs and on-screen captures of the opening sequence and every state into that directory. Without the variable the export test returns immediately. This is the intended way to audition the sounds and inspect the control at real size before judging it.

## Verification record

Environment: development Mac, macOS 26.6.2, Xcode 26.6, arm64 Debug with the existing local signing identity and existing capture/input permissions. No permissions or model files were changed.

- TDD red: the added interaction tests first failed to compile because `ReadingPanelState` and `ReadingStatusPresenting` did not exist.
- Initial green: 52 Swift Testing app tests and both live permission tests passed. Core: one Swift Testing test and all 11 behavior checks passed.
- Unit coverage includes hidden-before-capture behavior, selected display routing, loading/playback cancellation, rejection of old stop callbacks, completion/empty/error states, timer invalidation, nonactivation, disappearing terminal states, unknown displays and negative-origin display geometry.
- Initial native run: real OCR → Supertonic → global Escape and repeated-shortcut cancellation passed. The stop-click test failed because an `NSPanel` is exposed as a dialog, not a normal window. The content now has an explicit accessibility group for inspection.
- Second stop-click run reached the panel but failed because XCTest reported a non-finite application-root frame. The test now anchors its click to the observed fixture text element and asserts finite coordinates. These were test-harness failures, not passed stop-click results.
- An early screenshot omitted the panel because of its window-sharing flag. That flag was removed: the panel contains only public status, while the app's own capture continues excluding the entire app.
- Computer Use attachment to the exact Debug app returned `timeoutReached` (-10005). Native UI automation is separately authorized by the owner; no successful Computer Use session is claimed.
- Final-code Debug app tests: 52 Swift Testing tests and both live permission tests passed again. Release build passed; the only build warning was the existing skipped App Intents metadata extraction.
- The final-code combined UI run passed Escape, repeated-shortcut and stop-button cancellation, including foreground preservation during playback and after the stop click. Natural completion reached Ready and hid the panel but failed its final foreground-app assertion once. An earlier isolated completion run had passed; this combined run is **not** an all-green result.
- The exported final-code screenshot was visually inspected: the entire panel renders at 640 × 128 Retina pixels with readable, unclipped labels, mint speaker, opaque dark foundation and a 44 pt stop target. No screenshot or selected text is displayed in the panel.
- Two isolated natural-completion repetitions then passed (29.800 s and 29.484 s, zero skips), including Ready, hidden panel and retained fixture focus. The test now names the actual foreground application on assertion failure. The earlier intermittent foreground assertion has not been attributed to a proven cause; it remains recorded rather than erased by the rerun.

Local result bundles for this session: `/tmp/osr-panel-final.xcresult` (52 app tests + 2 live permission checks passed; 3/4 UI flows passed), `/tmp/osr-panel-completion-recheck.xcresult` (2/2 isolated completion repetitions passed). These are temporary development artifacts, not product history or committed screenshots. No full physical-display/accessibility regression is claimed.

## Redesign verification, 2026-09-05

Environment: development Mac, macOS 26.6.2, Xcode 26.6, arm64 Debug and Release builds with the existing local signing identity. No permissions or model files were changed.

- Test build and Release build passed with no warnings in the panel, sound or test sources (the only warning remains the pre-existing skipped App Intents metadata extraction).
- 58 Swift Testing app tests passed (`/tmp/osr-redesign-unit.xcresult`). New or updated coverage: the drawing clock does not retain the view (a display-link proxy is weak); the same native host survives recognizing → preparing → speaking and an immediate hide cancels the exit; all four cues are deterministic, normalised to their intended peak, start at zero, end below 0.001, stay under 800 ms (tap under 60 ms) and decode as mono WAV without files; a stop-button press plays the tap without registering an interruptible cue, so cancellation cannot clip it; panel geometry still fits negative-origin and tiny visible frames.
- The opt-in preview export ran against the real on-screen panel: 16 captures (opening sequence, recognizing, preparing, three speaking levels, finished drawing/settled/closing, stopped, no-text, failed) plus the four WAVs. The captures were visually inspected: translucent ink body with blur, glowing ribbon whose amplitude visibly follows the injected level, travelling light during recognition, drawn check/stop/amber symbols, round stop control, and readable unclipped labels after the body was widened from 288 to 312 pt (the first pass truncated "Text erkennen …" and "Esc zum Stoppen"). Cue analysis: peaks exactly 0.50/0.50/0.50/0.42, tails below 0.002, no sample discontinuities beyond the intended strike transients.
- **Real OCR → Supertonic → panel flows could not be verified in this session.** `testReadingPanelStopButtonPreservesOtherAppFocus` and `testReadingPanelDisappearsAfterNaturalCompletion` both failed with the status item at "Error" immediately after the drag, in a custom DerivedData path and in the default one. `LiveCaptureTests` confirmed the cause: Screen Recording and Input Monitoring are not granted to the current Debug app test host, so capture fails before the panel is ever shown. Re-grant both to the Debug app in System Settings and rerun those two tests; until then the stop click, foreground preservation and natural-completion dismissal of the redesigned control are covered only by unit tests and the preview export, not by the native flow.
- No Computer Use session and no hands-on audition by the owner is claimed. The owner's subjective acceptance of look, feel and sound remains open.
