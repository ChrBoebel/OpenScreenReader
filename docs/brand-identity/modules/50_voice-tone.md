## Synthesis

### Voice principles

1. **Plain before clever.** Use short, concrete language that describes an action or outcome.
2. **Calm confidence.** State verified capabilities directly; avoid hype, fear, and self-congratulation.
3. **Respectful agency.** Say what the app will do and let users choose. Never infantilize or presume a diagnosis.
4. **Specific transparency.** Explain permissions, network access, storage, and limitations at the moment they matter.
5. **Human, technically honest.** Translate technical details without hiding trade-offs or pretending provisional architecture is settled.

### Three candidate voice descriptions

1. **Quietly capable:** concise, reassuring through clarity, and never attention-seeking.
2. **Open by default:** direct about data flow, limitations, licenses, and user choices.
3. **Warmly precise:** accessible and human, while treating technical and accessibility claims with care.

Use all three as a system: quietly capable is the baseline, open by default governs trust moments, and warmly precise governs help and errors.

### Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product promise | Crisp and benefit-led | “Select any text. Hear it naturally. Keep it local.” |
| First launch | Welcoming and minimal | Explain the one flow before settings or technology. |
| Permission request | Candid and contextual | Explain why screen capture is needed and when it is used. |
| Model download | Factual and consent-led | Show model, source, size, license, destination, and integrity information. |
| Listening state | Nearly invisible | Small labels such as “Recognizing,” “Speaking,” and “Stopped.” |
| Error or cancellation | Neutral and actionable | Say what stopped, what was retained, and the next safe action. |
| Technical documentation | Precise and testable | Separate current behavior, target behavior, and hypotheses. |

### Do / avoid

- **Do:** “OCR and speech run locally after installation.”
- **Avoid:** “Your data is always 100% safe.”
- **Do:** “OpenScreenReader reads the region you select.”
- **Avoid:** “A revolutionary AI sees everything for you.”
- **Do:** “OpenScreenReader is not a complete replacement for VoiceOver.”
- **Avoid:** language that implies complete blind system navigation.
- **Do:** lead with listening and control; explain models only when relevant.
- **Avoid:** making “AI” the protagonist.

### Projective check: support response

The evidence-based brand response to a failed read would acknowledge the failure plainly, confirm that no cloud fallback occurred, preserve user control, and offer one specific recovery action. No fabricated support quote is recorded.

### Productive tensions

- Reassurance must come from evidence, not absolute security language.
- Technical candor must remain skimmable in a low-friction product.
- “Natural” is emotionally legible but must be validated as a product claim.
